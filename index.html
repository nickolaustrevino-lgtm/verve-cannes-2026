Positioning System: From Tagline to Messaging Architecture

The Problem With "Beyond Search. Into Thought."

The current tagline is evocative but lacks load-bearing structure. It names a directional idea without completing the buyer's question: so what does that mean for my budget, my measurement, or my campaign outcomes? At Cannes – surrounded by TTD, Google, and Amazon – every brand marketer will ask: "How is this different from what I already buy?" A tagline cannot answer that question. A messaging architecture can.

Recommended Three-Layer Messaging Architecture

L1

Category Definition – What You Are

Verve for Advertisers is the intent intelligence platform for the AI-native era – the only open-market solution that unifies what consumers search, what they consent to share, and what they ask AI – without cookies, device IDs, or walled garden dependencies.

This language names a new category ("intent intelligence platform for the AI-native era") rather than slotting Verve into an existing DSP/data provider frame, and anchors differentiation in the three-signal spine as an integrated system.
L2

ICP Problem Statement – What Buyers Lose Without You

Name the three forces fragmenting buyer intent in 2026:

  • Identity loss: IDFA deprecation and cookie collapse stripped targeting precision for open-web media
  • Discovery migration: Consumer consideration is structurally moving from browser-based search to AI-native interfaces, where traditional keyword intent signals go dark
  • Walled garden opacity: Platform-reported attribution from Google and Meta offers no external validation, no signal ownership, and no portability
Position Verve as the infrastructure layer that restores signal fidelity across all three failure modes – not an alternative DSP, but the intelligence layer that makes all other DSPs smarter.
L3

Proof Points – What You Can Actually Demonstrate

Each pillar of the three-signal spine needs a concrete, buyer-facing proof point translated into commercial language:

"1B+ daily intent signals across search, consent, and AI-native discovery"

"First and only platform to activate LLM conversational intent for programmatic targeting"

"ID-less performance at scale – no cookies, no IDFA required"

Competitive Wedge Table

The VP's job – not to describe what Verve does, but to make the alternative cost viscerally clear. This table gives sales the language to handle the most common competitive objection at Cannes without requiring a product demo.

Google / Meta Owned-channel performance Signal ownership, cross-web visibility, AI-era discovery data Open-web intelligence layer + LLM intent unavailable in walled gardens

PRE-EVENT: THE 30-DAY PRE-TARGETING WINDOW

The most common Cannes failure is treating the event as a walk-up opportunity. The VP-level move is to treat Cannes as the closing moment of a 30-day pre-event ABM sprint.

Named Account List

Work with sales 30 days out to produce a priority list of 50–75 named accounts attending Cannes – CMOs, Chief Media Officers, agency leads, and programmatic trading heads at ICP-matching companies. This is not a spray-and-pray registration list; it is accounts that sales has already touched or wants to break into.

Pre-Targeted LinkedIn ABM

Run ABM sequences (Thought Leader Ads, Message Ads, Document Ads with the LLM signal white paper) against the named account list for the 4 weeks before the festival. The content should tee up the "what are you doing about LLM-era intent?" question – not sell the platform.

Pre-Booked Meeting Calendar

The goal is to arrive at Cannes with 30+ confirmed meetings already in the calendar, not to "see who shows up." Every dinner, beach session, and stage activation is then a second touchpoint for a pre-warmed relationship, not a first.

🎯

ON-SITE CONVERSION: THREE ACTIVATION TIERS

Tier 1 – Private Intelligence Dinner (Mon & Wed Evening)

Limit to 12–16 attendees maximum. Curate by ICP role – no junior titles, no existing Verve clients. Each dinner organized around a singular provocation: "What happens to your intent data strategy when 40% of consumer discovery shifts to AI agents by 2028?" – not a product presentation. Assign one sales AE per 4 attendees. Every attendee leaves with a custom one-page intelligence brief on their category's LLM intent data exposure – produced pre-event and personalized.

Tier 2 – Beach Activation: AI Signal Observatory

Reframe the beach as a live demonstration environment – an "AI Signal Observatory" where visitors see an anonymized, real-time view of how intent signals move across search, consent, and LLM environments. Scheduled "signal briefings" every 90 minutes run by a Verve data scientist, not a sales rep. Visitors register via a QR-to-meeting intake form that directly feeds the CRM. Limit capacity per session to create scarcity and qualification.

Tier 3 – Stage / Panel: Category Authority, Not Product Demo

Verve should not be presenting about its product on stage. It should be presenting the category problem that its product solves. Proposed talk title: "The Intent Gap: What Advertisers Are Missing When 40% of Consideration Moves to AI." Invite a brand-side CMO and an agency trading lead to co-present – creating commercial credibility and expanding audience draw. Submit for a Palais stage slot or host a curated content session.

⚡

POST-EVENT: THE 72-HOUR CLOSE WINDOW

Best-in-class B2B event operators close pipeline in the 72 hours after the event, not the 10 business days. Build this into the operational plan before Cannes, not after.

24-Hour Follow-Up

Personalized email from the assigned AE within 24 hours, referencing the specific conversation point. Not a template – a sentence or two anchored in something said at dinner or on the beach.

Intelligence Brief Delivery

The custom one-page category brief is sent as a follow-up asset, giving the contact something to share internally – the leave-behind that keeps working after Cannes.

Second Meeting Offer

Every follow-up includes a specific, time-bound offer: "We're running a 45-minute LLM intent audit for five accounts in July; I'd like to offer you one of those slots."

CRM Tagging & Pipeline Update

All Cannes contacts logged in CRM within 48 hours, tagged by activation tier (dinner / beach / stage), and moved to an appropriate pipeline stage. No contact stays in "event lead" status past 5 business days.

Objection Handling Architecture

The most common objection at Cannes for a two-month-old brand: "I've heard about LLM intent but I'm not sure it's ready for scaled activation." Pre-build the rebuttal architecture before the event.

Objection: "Is this actually live or still in testing?"

Anchor: "The platform activated conversational intent signals from major LLM environments on March 10, 2026 – it's live and available to advertisers today. We're already running it."

Bridge: "The more relevant question is what signals you're missing right now while you're evaluating. Can we look at your current intent coverage for [their category]?"

Objection: "What about privacy and data provenance?"

Answer: Pseudonymized behavioral patterns, no raw message content stored, opted-in users from LLM applications. The privacy architecture is not a compliance footnote – it is the product's structural advantage. Privacy-first methodology generates richer signals, not fewer.

Objection: "We already use Captify."

Answer: The new Verve offering integrates Captify's search data with LLM and zero-party signals into a unified activation layer – it is categorically different from the standalone data product. You are not buying Captify; you are buying Captify made smarter by two additional signal layers, directly activated.

Objection: "What about scale?"

Answer: 1B+ daily intent signals across search, consent, and AI-native discovery. The platform processed over one billion daily signals from day one of the LLM signal launch. Scale is not the question – unique signal quality is.

📊 Efficiency

Cost per qualified meeting; cost per opportunity created. Transforms Cannes from a "brand investment" into a managed acquisition channel with a defensible 4:1 pipeline-to-cost ratio over a 90-day post-event attribution window.

🔄 Conversion

Meetings → opportunities → closed-won pipeline influenced. Tracks the full funnel from first on-site conversation to booked revenue, with 90-day attribution window.

📣 Category SOV

Share of voice at event: speaking slots secured, press mentions, social conversation volume around "conversational intent." Tracks whether Verve is defining the category or just participating in it.

✅ Enablement

Percentage of Cannes-originated contacts who received sales follow-up within 48 hours. The single clearest proxy for whether the marketing-to-sales handoff is functioning as designed.

Executive-Level Ownership: Closing the Consultant-to-Operator Gap

A consultant frames possibilities. A VP operator commits to outcomes. The specific upgrades that close this gap:

📋

Replace "Consider" Language with Owned Commitments

Every activation, every pipeline metric, and every follow-up SLA should have a named owner and a date. The Cannes section of the 90-day plan should read like an operational spec, not a strategic recommendation. If a deliverable does not have an owner and a due date, it does not exist.

Deliverable: Rewrite the Cannes activation plan with named owners, due dates, and SLAs for every component before final sign-off.
📈

Build a Cannes Dashboard Before the Event

The VP presents a one-page Cannes scorecard to the CBO before the festival and delivers a post-event version within 5 business days of return. The scorecard tracks: meetings held vs. target; opportunities created; cost per qualified meeting; named accounts penetrated; press/analyst conversations; content assets shipped for post-event use.

This artifact demonstrates VP-level accountability – it is the difference between owning the outcome and reporting on activities.
🔗

Own the Post-Cannes Narrative Internally

Within 2 weeks of Cannes, the VP presents a "Cannes Debrief + Pipeline Outlook" to sales leadership and the CBO – covering what worked, what the pipeline impact was, and what the 30-day follow-up playbook is. This closes the loop from event investment to revenue outcome and establishes the VP as the connective tissue between marketing and revenue.

The internal debrief is as important as the event itself. It's how marketing earns ongoing investment authority from the revenue leadership team.
📊

Separate Brand KPIs from Pipeline KPIs in Reporting

A common VP failure mode is reporting event outcomes in a mixed metric soup – impressions, attendee counts, and pipeline numbers combined in a way that makes accountability impossible. Maintain two separate scorecards for Cannes:

Brand / Narrative Scorecard

Share of voice, press coverage, category vocabulary adoption ("conversational intent" mentions), stage appearances

Pipeline Scorecard

Meetings, opportunities created, influenced revenue, cost per qualified meeting, 90-day pipeline-to-cost ratio

Both matter. Conflating them makes neither defensible to the CBO or CFO.

The One Decision That Signals VP Ownership

Commit to a named account list before Cannes and hold the marketing function accountable for meeting penetration rate on that list. Not total meetings attended, not badge scans – named accounts with a first or second meeting. This makes the event strategy directly legible to the CBO and CEO as a revenue investment, not an awareness play.
