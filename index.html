2023 — Brand-Building & Presence

Verve Group had a low-key but consistent presence — hospitality events, networking meetups, and relationship-building along the Croisette. The primary narrative was establishing Verve as a legitimate scale player in mobile and CTV supply-side infrastructure. No transformative announcement; more of a "meet us" moment.

2024 — Acquisition as the Main Event

Cannes 2024 was a watershed moment — but not by design. Verve announced the acquisition of Jun Group for $185 million right during the festival week (June 18–19). This made Verve a deal-of-the-week story in the trade press, with the narrative being: Verve becomes a more balanced demand+supply company with direct access to 230+ Fortune 500 advertisers and agencies. The Cannes presence became a de facto acquisition roadshow. The story was about scale and integration, not a distinct product narrative.

2025 — AI Benchmarking

Verve leaned into the Cannes 2025 industry narrative that AI moved "from buzzword to benchmark." Verve contributed thought leadership in Performance Marketing World and eMarketer commentary, framing AI as a utility-first infrastructure story — not hype. Industry reactions quoted Verve executives as part of a broader chorus of ad tech voices on AI's mainstreaming. It was still a reactive, amplification-focused play rather than a defining moment.

The Differentiated 2026 Positioning

"Beyond Search. Into Thought."

The Core Idea

Every other ad tech player is still fighting over search intent — what someone typed into Google. Verve For Advertisers now captures conversational intent — the higher-fidelity signal of what someone is actively reasoning about inside AI chat environments. This is a fundamentally new class of demand signal. The positioning opportunity is clear:

"We don't just know what consumers searched for.
We know what they're thinking about deciding."

Why This Is Differentiated at Cannes 2026

The table below maps the competitive messaging landscape — and where Verve's narrative creates genuine white space.

What Everyone Else Will Say What Verve Should Say
"AI makes our targeting smarter" "We're the first to target based on what people are deciding inside AI"
"We use AI to optimize campaigns" "We capture intent signals born inside AI chat — before your competitor even knows the consumer exists"
"Privacy-first audience targeting" "Privacy-first + the next generation of intent — LLM signals, zero-party data, and search unified into one layer"
"AI as a tool for creative" "AI as a signal source — the demand side of the AI revolution"
The key contrast: competitors will talk about using AI to make ads better. Verve's story is about AI changing where consumer intent lives — and being the only platform already tapping it.

Strategic Positioning Pillars

Four strategic pillars anchor the Verve For Advertisers Cannes 2026 narrative. Each pillar addresses a distinct conversation happening on the Croisette.

01

The Intent Economy Has Moved

Consumer discovery has structurally shifted from search bars to AI chat environments. LLM signals are the new search cookie — representing the next evolution of the highest-value signal in advertising. Verve is the only open-market platform already live on this.

Why It Lands at Cannes: Directly addresses the "what comes after cookies" anxiety that has dominated adtech strategy for three years — but with a forward-looking answer, not a defensive one.
02

One Intelligence Layer

Zero-party consent signals (Jun Group) + the world's largest open-web search intent dataset (Captify) + conversational AI signals (LLM launch) — unified into a single activation platform. This is not a point solution; it's a convergence play that makes the whole smarter than any individual data source.

Why It Lands at Cannes: In a fragmented ecosystem, "unified intelligence" is a genuinely rare claim. Competitors either have data or media — Verve is the spine that connects both.
03

Privacy-First ≠ Signal-Poor

The dominant industry narrative has been that privacy deprecation means signal loss. Verve's counter-narrative: responsible methodology generates richer, more durable signals — not fewer. The privacy architecture isn't a constraint; it's a competitive moat.

Why It Lands at Cannes: Cannes 2026 will feature dozens of conversations about signal loss and identity resolution anxiety. Verve enters those conversations with a positive counter-narrative rather than a defensive posture.
04

Already Live. Not a Roadmap.

The LLM signal technology launched March 10, 2026. Cannes 2026 becomes a proof-point event — where Verve shows, not announces. In a sea of AI roadmap presentations and vaporware demos, Verve's live, demonstrated capability is a fundamental differentiator.

Why It Lands at Cannes: The 2026 Cannes AI & Tech Sandbox and the new Creative Data Lion both reward demonstrated capability. Verve can walk into any room on the Croisette with a working demo — not a slide deck.

Competitive Differentiation

Verve For Advertisers occupies a unique position in the competitive landscape — distinct from pure DSPs, data vendors, walled gardens, and mobile performance players.

Competitor Category How Verve Differs
The Trade Desk, Xandr Invest, DV360 Verve leans heavily into proprietary multi-source intent + ID-less performance; less of a pure generic DSP and more of a data-plus-activation spine. Where these platforms provide pipes, Verve provides intelligence.

Amazon Ads, Meta Ads Verve positions itself as the outside-the-walled-gardens intelligence layer — complementing, not replacing, those buys. Verve provides cross-site, cross-app, and AI-search visibility that walled gardens structurally cannot offer.
